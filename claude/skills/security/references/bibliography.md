# Bibliography — security

Canonical sources for content not covered by the vault (STRIDE, password storage, secure design).
Status: [verified] = citation checked online; [known] = standard citation, not re-checked online (no web search used for this skill).

## Threat modeling
- [known] Shostack, A. (2014). *Threat Modeling: Designing for Security*. Wiley. STRIDE and data-flow-diagram based modeling.
- [known] Kohnfelder, L., & Garg, P. (1999). The Threats to Our Products. Microsoft Interface (origin of STRIDE).

## Application security
- [known] OWASP Foundation. *OWASP Top 10 (2021)*. https://owasp.org/Top10 (also in the vault note).
- [known] OWASP Application Security Verification Standard (ASVS). https://owasp.org/www-project-application-security-verification-standard/
- [known] OWASP Cheat Sheet Series (Password Storage, Input Validation, Logging). https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org

## Secure design principles
- [known] Saltzer, J. H., & Schroeder, M. D. (1975). The Protection of Information in Computer Systems. *Proceedings of the IEEE*, 63(9), 1278–1308. Least privilege, fail-safe defaults, complete mediation, economy of mechanism, open design, separation of privilege.

## Cryptography and keys
- [known] Ferguson, N., Schneier, B., & Kohno, T. (2010). *Cryptography Engineering*. Wiley.
- [known] NIST SP 800-57 Part 1. *Recommendation for Key Management*.
- [known] NIST SP 800-63B. *Digital Identity Guidelines: Authentication and Lifecycle Management* (password and MFA guidance).

## Risk, incidents, regulation
- [known] ISO/IEC 27001 and 27002; ISO/IEC 31000; NIST SP 800-30; NIST SP 800-61 (incident handling).
- [known] Ley 25.326 (Protección de Datos Personales, Argentina); Ley 26.388 (Delitos Informáticos); Ley 25.506 (Firma Digital).
