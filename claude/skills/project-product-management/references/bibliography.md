# Bibliography — project-product-management

Canonical sources for content not covered by the vault (scope/schedule/cost, group dynamics, ethics, economics).
Status: [verified] = citation checked online; [known] = standard citation, not re-checked online.

## Scope, schedule, cost, risk (knowledge areas)
- [known] Project Management Institute. *A Guide to the Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK Guide)*. PMI. Knowledge areas: scope, schedule, cost, risk (ch. 11), procurement (ch. 12), communications, resources.
- [known] Brooks, F. P. (1975). *The Mythical Man-Month*. Addison-Wesley. Estimation optimism, adding people to a late project.
- [known] McConnell, S. (2006). *Software Estimation: Demystifying the Black Art*. Microsoft Press. Estimates as ranges; cone of uncertainty.
- [known] Boehm, B. W. (1981). *Software Engineering Economics*. Prentice Hall. Cost estimation (COCOMO).

## Team dynamics, feedback, conflict
- [known] Tuckman, B. W. (1965). Developmental sequence in small groups. *Psychological Bulletin*, 63(6), 384–399. Forming, storming, norming, performing.
- [known] DeMarco, T., & Lister, T. (1987). *Peopleware*. Dorset House. Teams and environment in software projects.
- [known] Stone, D., Patton, B., & Heen, S. (1999). *Difficult Conversations*. Viking. Giving feedback and handling conflict.

## Ethics
- [known] ACM/IEEE-CS. *Software Engineering Code of Ethics and Professional Practice* (1999). Public interest, honesty, professional judgment.
- [known] ACM (2018). *ACM Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct*. Privacy, honoring licenses and IP, honesty.

## Economics for decisions
- [known] Mankiw, N. G. *Principles of Economics*. Cengage. Opportunity cost, marginal and cost/benefit reasoning.
