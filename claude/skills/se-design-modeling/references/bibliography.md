# Bibliography — se-design-modeling

Canonical sources for content not covered by the vault (GRASP, coupling/cohesion, responsibilities).
Status: [verified] = citation checked online on 2026-11-19; [known] = standard citation, not re-checked online.

## Responsibility assignment (GRASP)
- [verified] Larman, C. (2004). *Applying UML and Patterns: An Introduction to Object-Oriented Analysis and Design and Iterative Development* (3rd ed.). Prentice Hall / Pearson. ISBN 0-13-148906-2. Ch. 17 "GRASP: Designing Objects with Responsibilities"; Ch. 18 "Object Design Examples with GRASP". Defines nine principles: Information Expert, Creator, Controller, Low Coupling, High Cohesion, Polymorphism, Pure Fabrication, Indirection, Protected Variations.

## Coupling and cohesion
- [verified] Stevens, W. P., Myers, G. J., & Constantine, L. L. (1974). Structured Design. *IBM Systems Journal*, 13(2), 115–139. Origin of the published coupling/cohesion vocabulary; functional cohesion is the strongest form.
- [known] Parnas, D. L. (1972). On the Criteria to Be Used in Decomposing Systems into Modules. *Communications of the ACM*, 15(12), 1053–1058. Information hiding as the decomposition criterion (basis of Protected Variations).

## Responsibilities and CRC
- [verified] Beck, K., & Cunningham, W. (1989). A Laboratory for Teaching Object-Oriented Thinking. *OOPSLA '89 Proceedings*, 1–6. Introduces CRC cards (Class, Responsibilities, Collaborators).
- [known] Wirfs-Brock, R., & Wilkerson, B. (1989). Object-Oriented Design: A Responsibility-Driven Approach. *OOPSLA '89 Proceedings*, 71–75. Origin of Responsibility-Driven Design (RDD).

## Patterns
- [known] Gamma, E., Helm, R., Johnson, R., & Vlissides, J. (1994). *Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software*. Addison-Wesley. (GoF catalogue: observer, facade, iterator, decorator…)
