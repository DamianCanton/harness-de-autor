# Bibliography — se-process-requirements

Canonical sources for content not covered by the vault (stakeholders, information systems, requirement quality, use-case/user-story practice).
Status: [verified] = citation checked online; [known] = standard citation, not re-checked online (no web search was available when this file was written, 2026-11-19).

## Process models
- [known] Royce, W. W. (1970). Managing the Development of Large Software Systems. *Proceedings of IEEE WESCON*, 328–338. Source of the waterfall diagram (Royce himself argued for iteration).
- [known] Boehm, B. W. (1988). A Spiral Model of Software Development and Enhancement. *IEEE Computer*, 21(5), 61–72. Risk-driven spiral.
- [known] Kruchten, P. (2003). *The Rational Unified Process: An Introduction* (3rd ed.). Addison-Wesley.
- [known] Beck, K., et al. (2001). *Manifesto for Agile Software Development*. agilemanifesto.org.
- [known] Schwaber, K., & Sutherland, J. *The Scrum Guide*. scrumguides.org (current edition).
- [known] Beck, K. (2004). *Extreme Programming Explained* (2nd ed.). Addison-Wesley.
- [known] Anderson, D. J. (2010). *Kanban: Successful Evolutionary Change for Your Technology Business*. Blue Hole Press.
- [known] Sommerville, I. (2011). *Software Engineering* (9th ed.). Pearson. Process models and requirements engineering chapters (the course reading source).

## Requirements engineering
- [known] IEEE Std 830-1998. *Recommended Practice for Software Requirements Specifications* (superseded by ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018).
- [known] ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018. *Systems and software engineering — Life cycle processes — Requirements engineering*. Requirement quality characteristics (necessary, unambiguous, verifiable, consistent, complete).
- [known] Robertson, S., & Robertson, J. (2012). *Mastering the Requirements Process* (3rd ed.). Addison-Wesley. Elicitation, Volere shell, stakeholder analysis.
- [known] Wiegers, K., & Beatty, J. (2013). *Software Requirements* (3rd ed.). Microsoft Press.

## Use cases and user stories
- [known] Cockburn, A. (2001). *Writing Effective Use Cases*. Addison-Wesley. Goal-level naming, preconditions/postconditions, main and extension flows.
- [known] Jacobson, I., et al. (1992). *Object-Oriented Software Engineering: A Use Case Driven Approach*. Addison-Wesley. Origin of use cases, include/extend.
- [known] Cohn, M. (2004). *User Stories Applied*. Addison-Wesley.
- [known] Wake, B. (2003). INVEST in Good Stories, and SMART Tasks. xp123.com.

## Information systems and stakeholders (canonical)
- [known] Laudon, K. C., & Laudon, J. P. *Management Information Systems: Managing the Digital Firm* (current edition). Pearson. System types (TPS, MIS, DSS, ERP) and IS in the organization.
- [known] Freeman, R. E. (1984). *Strategic Management: A Stakeholder Approach*. Pitman. Stakeholder concept.
