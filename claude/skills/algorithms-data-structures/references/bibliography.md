## Textbooks
- [verified] Cormen, T. H., Leiserson, C. E., Rivest, R. L., & Stein, C. (2022). *Introduction to Algorithms* (4th ed.). MIT Press. ISBN 978-0-262-04630-5. Complexity analysis, data structures, sorting, graphs, amortized analysis (Ch. 16).
- [known] Knuth, D. E. (1997–1998). *The Art of Computer Programming*, Vols. 1 (Fundamental Algorithms, 3rd ed.) and 3 (Sorting and Searching, 2nd ed.). Addison-Wesley.
- [known] Sedgewick, R., & Wayne, K. (2011). *Algorithms* (4th ed.). Addison-Wesley.

## Asymptotic notation
- [verified] Knuth, D. E. (1976). Big Omicron and Big Omega and Big Theta. *ACM SIGACT News*, 8(2), 18–24. DOI 10.1145/1008328.1008329. O as upper bound, Ω lower bound, Θ exact order.

## Invariants and correctness
- [verified] Hoare, C. A. R. (1969). An Axiomatic Basis for Computer Programming. *Communications of the ACM*, 12(10), 576–580, 583. DOI 10.1145/363235.363259. Pre/postconditions and the while-loop invariant rule.

## Amortized cost
- [verified] Tarjan, R. E. (1985). Amortized Computational Complexity. *SIAM Journal on Algebraic and Discrete Methods*, 6(2), 306–318. DOI 10.1137/0606031. Amortized running time as a robust complexity measure; potential method.

## Sorting
- [known] Hoare, C. A. R. (1962). Quicksort. *The Computer Journal*, 5(1), 10–16.
