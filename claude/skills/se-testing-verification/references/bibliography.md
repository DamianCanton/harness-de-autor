# Bibliography — se-testing-verification

Canonical sources for content not covered by the vault (ISO/IEC 25010 quality model, process quality, logic applied to code).
Status: [verified] = citation checked online; [known] = standard citation, not re-checked online (no web search was available when this file was written).

## Software quality (canonical)
- [known] ISO/IEC 25010:2011. *Systems and software engineering — Systems and software Quality Requirements and Evaluation (SQuaRE) — System and software quality models*. Product quality model: functional suitability, performance efficiency, compatibility, usability, reliability, security, maintainability, portability (2011 edition; the 2023 revision changes some names, e.g. adds safety).
- [known] ISO 9001 (quality management systems) — process quality; see vault TIS §7.5 for the course treatment.
- [known] Sommerville, I. (2011). *Software Engineering* (9th ed.). Addison-Wesley. Quality management, inspections, metrics, V&V, TDD (the vault's source).

## Testing
- [known] Ammann, P., & Offutt, J. (2016). *Introduction to Software Testing* (2nd ed.). Cambridge University Press. Input-space partitioning, coverage criteria, subsumption.
- [known] Beck, K. (2002). *Test-Driven Development: By Example*. Addison-Wesley.
- [known] Myers, G. J. (1979). *The Art of Software Testing*. Wiley. Boundary values, equivalence classes.
- [known] Barr, E. T., Harman, M., McMinn, P., Shahbaz, M., & Yoo, S. (2015). The Oracle Problem in Software Testing: A Survey. *IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering*, 41(5), 507–525.
- [known] Claessen, K., & Hughes, J. (2000). QuickCheck: a lightweight tool for random testing of Haskell programs. *ICFP 2000*.
- [known] Buxton, J. N., & Randell, B. (Eds.) (1970). *Software Engineering Techniques* (NATO Science Committee report). Source of Dijkstra's "presence, not absence of bugs".

## Formal verification
- [known] Hoare, C. A. R. (1969). An Axiomatic Basis for Computer Programming. *Communications of the ACM*, 12(10), 576–580.
- [known] Dijkstra, E. W. (1975). Guarded commands, nondeterminacy and formal derivation of programs. *Communications of the ACM*, 18(8), 453–457. Weakest precondition.
- [known] Meyer, B. (1992). Applying "Design by Contract". *IEEE Computer*, 25(10), 40–51.
- [known] Jackson, D. (2012). *Software Abstractions: Logic, Language, and Analysis* (revised ed.). MIT Press. Alloy and the small scope hypothesis.

## Logic and discrete mathematics applied to code (canonical)
- [known] Rosen, K. H. (2019). *Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications* (8th ed.). McGraw-Hill. Predicates, quantifiers, induction.
- [known] Huth, M., & Ryan, M. (2004). *Logic in Computer Science: Modelling and Reasoning about Systems* (2nd ed.). Cambridge University Press. Propositional and predicate logic, program verification.
